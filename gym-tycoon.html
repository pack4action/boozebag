How it works

Placing gear. Click a piece below to pick it up, nudge it with the arrows or just drag it, then hit the tick. It lands wherever you left it, not on a grid square. Click a placed piece to move it again or store it. Only what is on the floor earns anything, and gear in your inventory sells back for 60% of what it cost.

Arrangement. Two pieces of the same category -- they are colour-coded -- standing within about two metres of each other are worth +12% each. A Gear Fridge or Hype Sound System next to something from a different category is worth +20%.

Rush orders. From level 3 the board carries one timed order at a time: three times the pay of a standing job, eight minutes to do it in, and it only ever asks for something you can buy or place on the spot. Miss one and the board goes quiet for twenty minutes; finish one and the next comes sooner.

Fittings. A palm, a water cooler, a mirror wall or a neon sign earns nothing on its own. It raises the room's vibe, which lifts everything else in the room and draws a bigger crowd. Each one takes a slot a machine could have had, which is the trade.

Rooms and themes. Garage, Basement and Rooftop are three separate gyms, each growing its own chain of rooms joined by hallways, and each laid out differently -- the garage is a row of wide vehicle bays, the basement a chain of narrow cells, the rooftop a spread of open decks. Gear stays where you put it when you switch. Drag or swipe to pan; pinch, or hold ctrl and scroll, to zoom.

Levels. Every piece you buy earns experience and better kit earns more. Levelling up is what opens the Basement, the Rooftop and the office tier.

Jobs. Three standing requests beside the shop, with the money and experience for doing them stated up front. Finish one and another takes its place.

Staff. Hired for cash, then paid a share of what the gym takes. Each extra hire in a role is worth less than the one before while the wage stays the same, so there is a point past which the next one costs more than they bring in. Anyone can be let go.

Rush hours. The gym fills up in the morning and again in the evening, on your own clock, and everything earns a bonus while it lasts. A quiet hour is never worse than normal.

Franchising. Once the shop runs out, cash the whole gym in for points that multiply everything from then on. You keep your level and everything it unlocked.
