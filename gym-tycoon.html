Click a piece of gear below to pick it up, nudge it around the floor with the on-screen arrows (or just drag it), then hit the tick to set it down -- it lands wherever you left it, not on a fixed grid square. Click a placed piece to pick it up again, or store it away; only what's on the floor earns anything. Stand two of the same category (color-coded) within about two metres of each other for +12% each, or park a Personal Trainer, Gear Fridge, or Hype Sound System next to something from a different category for +20%. Garage, Basement, and Rooftop are completely separate empires, each with its own chain of connected rooms -- gear stays where you put it when you switch theme. Every theme is laid out differently: the garage is a row of wide, shallow vehicle bays, the basement a chain of narrow cells strung together by tunnels, and the rooftop a spread of open decks joined by walkways. Each starts at 12 slots and every room you buy after it is bigger, and every one is joined to the last by a hallway, so a floor plan turns corners as it grows. Drag or swipe to pan around it, and pinch to zoom -- two fingers on a phone or trackpad, ctrl and the scroll wheel with a mouse. Gear sitting unplaced in your inventory can be sold back for 60% of what it cost. Every piece you buy earns experience -- better kit earns more -- and levelling up is what opens the Basement, the Rooftop and the office tier. The jobs board beside the shop always has three things worth doing, with the money and experience for doing them stated up front; finish one and another takes its place.
