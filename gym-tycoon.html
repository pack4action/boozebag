Pick a piece of gear below and it lands in your hands in the middle of the room. Drag it where you want it, nudge it a hair at a time with the four arrows, and hit the green tick to set it down -- or the red cross to put it back. Tap anything already on the floor to pick it up and move it again, and use the bin to pack it away. Machines sit anywhere, down to a sixteenth of a tile, so you can line them up along a wall or angle them off the grid however you like; they just cannot overlap each other or hang off the edge. Leave equipment close together for a synergy bonus: two of the same category (colour-coded) near each other is +12% each, and a Personal Trainer, Steroid Cycle, Second Location or office hire standing near something different is +20%. Garage, Basement and Rooftop are completely separate empires, each with its own chain of connected rooms, and gear stays where you put it when you switch. Every theme is laid out differently -- the garage a row of wide, shallow bays, the basement a chain of deep cells strung together by tunnels, the rooftop a spread of open decks joined by walkways -- and every room you buy is bigger than the last, joined to it by a hallway, so the floor plan turns corners as it grows. Drag or swipe the background to pan, and pinch to zoom: two fingers on a phone or trackpad, ctrl and the scroll wheel with a mouse. Gear sitting unplaced in your inventory can be sold back for 60% of what it cost.

Progress saves automatically to this browser, but gains only tick up while this tab is open and on screen -- close it or switch away and the gym stops earning until you are back. Connect a wallet to put your total on the leaderboard.
