How it works

The loop. Buy a machine. It goes to Storage. Put it on the floor. Tap the bubble over it to take the money. Everything else makes that bubble fill faster.

Opening up. Take the free Customer Desk from the Shop and put it down. Nothing earns and nothing sells until it is. The desk itself earns nothing. Each location gets its own, free, and it can never be stored or sold.

Bubbles. Nothing pays into your balance by itself. A machine fills a bubble, and you tap it. A bubble holds about two minutes of takings; full bubble, machine stops. Upgrade the desk to make them hold more, up to an hour.

Cashiers. Hired into a room, up to three. They walk from bubble to bubble and take everything in each one they reach. More cashiers, faster rounds. Rooms you are not looking at still get their rounds.

Placing. Click a piece in Storage. Drag it, or use the arrow keys. R turns it. Enter places it. Escape puts it back. Place one and the next of the same kind is already in your hand. Two fingers pan and zoom while you hold something.

Clear floor. Each machine needs a strip of floor on the side you step on from. It shows as a dashed patch while you hold the piece. Nothing can stand in it.

Arrangement. Same category within two metres: +12% each. Decor takes no part in it. Room details, under the plan, shows what your layout earns.

Decor. Only gym equipment earns money. Decor earns nothing and each piece does one thing instead. A palm raises the room's vibe. A cooler makes its bubbles hold more. A mirror makes busy hours pay more there. A fridge speeds up its counters. A sound system means the room is never quiet. Neon makes promos last longer. A desk cuts wages, a cubicle raises job pay, and an office pod adds a cashier to every room. The last three count once for the whole gym.

Vibe. How nice a room is to train in. It lifts everything the room earns, and it fills a bar in Room details. There is a cap.

Upgrades. Own a machine and its shop row has an Upgrade button. Each tier is worth 2.2 times the last. Three tiers.

The counter. A Juice Bar or Pro Shop earns nothing. What it does is make stock. Start batches on the Counter tab. They run whether you are here or not. Delivery orders pay double for the stock.

Jobs. Three at a time, pay stated up front. From level 3, one rush order at a time on an eight-minute clock, worth triple.

Levels. Buying and upgrading gear earns XP. Levels open locations, staff and the bigger machines. Nothing else does.

Other staff. Cleaners lift vibe. Receptionists make busy hours busier. A Floor Manager adds to everything. These work the whole gym, and each extra hire does less. Anyone can be let go.

Busy hours. A day in the gym is an hour of real time, and the clock on the plan shows it. Mornings and evenings earn up to +20%. Quiet hours are never a penalty. The meter is in Room details. A promo is the same on demand: x2.5 for 90 seconds, then a 15-minute wait.

More rooms. The next room is marked out on the plan with a sign. Tap the sign to buy it.

Locations. Four separate gyms, each unlocked by level. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Franchising. When the shop runs out, cash the gym in for points that multiply everything after. You keep your level and trophies.

Saves on its own. Earns only while this tab is open. Counter batches keep running either way.
